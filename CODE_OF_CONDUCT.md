# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's or organization's secrets,
  infrastructure details, logs, or personal information.
- Help keep technical discussions focused and reproducible.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- insults, intimidation, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- encouraging unauthorized access, destructive operations, or credential
  exposure;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through an
appropriate private repository contact channel. Security reports must use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers should protect reporter privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and
apply consequences proportionally and consistently.
